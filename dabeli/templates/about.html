We Are Dinesh Dabeli

Dinesh Dabeli began in 2011 as a humble roadside fast-food stall beside DRB College in New City Light, Udhana. Built through the dedication of the Dubey family, our little stall grew one happy customer at a time.

In 2016, we opened our Bhimrad branch and added Vadapav alongside our signature Dabeli. By 2023, we had moved from a stall to permanent shops and continued taking the same authentic Surati taste to more neighbourhoods.

Today, Dinesh Dabeli proudly serves Surat through five locations, including Aagam Arcade and Green Arcade, Shop No. 2. Every branch is guided by the same passion for fresh, flavourful street food and warm service.
